# Leccy Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Leccy ("the App") handles information.

## 1. Summary

Leccy is designed as a local-first app. The developer of Leccy does not collect, sell, rent, track, or share your personal data through an account system, analytics system, advertising system, or cloud sync system.

Your notes, files, recordings, drawings, images, videos, flashcards, slides, and other study materials are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export, share, import, or send content to an external service.

## 2. Information Stored On Your Device

Leccy may store the following locally on your device:

- Notes and document content.
- Sheets, graph data, slides, and flashcards.
- Drawings and drawing exports.
- Imported images, videos, audio recordings, and files.
- Links you add to your notes.
- App settings and local database files.
- Gemini API key or related AI settings, if the App provides local storage for them.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to export it, share it, delete it, move it, back it up, or use a feature that sends selected content to a third-party service.

## 3. Gemini AI Features

Leccy may include optional AI features powered by Google's Gemini API. When you use those features, the content you choose to process may be sent to Google so Gemini can generate a response.

Examples may include:

- Text selected for restructuring.
- Notes used to generate slides.
- Notes used to generate flashcards.
- Prompts or instructions you enter.
- Gemini responses returned to the App.

The developer of Leccy does not receive this content through a separate server. However, Google may process the content according to Google's own Gemini API terms, privacy policy, billing status, logging settings, and data-use rules.

Do not use Gemini features with sensitive, confidential, private, medical, legal, financial, or restricted information unless you understand and accept how Google may process that information.

## 4. Microphone, Audio, Images, Videos, and Files

Leccy may request access to your microphone, files, images, or videos so you can insert or create content inside your notes.

The developer does not collect these files. They are used inside the App and stored locally unless you choose to export, share, or send them to a third-party service.

## 5. Internet Access

Leccy may use internet access for optional features such as:

- Calling Gemini API.
- Opening links you add.
- Loading third-party content if you choose to open or insert external links.

The developer does not use internet access for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## 6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or share your personal data.

Data may leave your device only when:

- You export or share a file.
- You open a link or external resource.
- You use Gemini or another third-party service.
- Your operating system, device backup, cloud backup, antivirus, file sync, or app store service processes local files according to its own settings.

## 7. Data Deletion

You can delete your data by deleting content inside the App, deleting exported files, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the App. Because Leccy stores data locally, the developer cannot remotely access, recover, or delete your local files.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Leccy is not intended for children under 13. Gemini API usage may have additional age or eligibility requirements under Google's terms.

## 9. Security

Leccy is designed to keep your content local, but no app, device, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, backups, exported files, and API keys.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will apply when it is made available.
